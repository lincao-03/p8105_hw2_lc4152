The combined dataset contains 1269 observations from Mr. Trash Wheel,
Professor Trash Wheel, and Gwynnda. Each observation represents one
dumpster collection. Key variables include the collection date, weight
in tons, volume in cubic yards, and counts of items such as plastic
bottles and cigarette butts. I cleaned the column names, removed rows
without dumpster-specific data, standardized the year and month
variables, and rounded sports balls to integers. I also added a variable
identifying each trash wheel before combining the datasets. Professor
Trash Wheel collected a total of 294.96 tons of trash, and Gwynnda
collected 1.812^{4} cigarette butts in June 2022.
