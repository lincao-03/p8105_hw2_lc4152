Professor Trash Wheel, and Gwynnda. Each observation represents one
dumpster collection. Key variables include the collection date, weight
in tons, volume in cubic yards, and counts of items such as plastic

The baseline file contains 483 participants. I skipped the first
descriptive row, cleaned the column names, and treated periods as
missing values. I excluded four participants whose MCI onset age was
less than or equal to their baseline age, leaving 479 eligible
